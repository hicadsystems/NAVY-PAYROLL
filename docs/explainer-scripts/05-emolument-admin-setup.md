# NARRATION ONLY

> Paste this block straight into your voice or avatar tool.

As Emolument Administrator you run the exercise. This first video covers the setup: opening the form window, deciding who holds which approving role, and getting your ships and personnel records right before anyone starts filling.

Sign in and open the admin dashboard. The top of the screen gives you the whole exercise in four numbers: Total Personnel, Not Filed, In Pipeline, and Confirmed by Central Pay Officer. Beside them, Overall Completion. Below, Ship Stats and Fleet Rank — ship-by-ship completion, ordered so the ships falling behind rise to the top. That is your daily working view once a cycle is running. Your left menu runs the full set: Dashboard, Role Management, Progress Report, Accept Verified Forms, My Form, Personnel Management, Ships Overview, Review & Approve, Control System, Payroll Update, View Tickets, and Audit Trail.

Start with Control System. This is where a form cycle is opened and closed, and nothing else can happen until you do. Create a cycle for the year, choose the form type — Officers, Ratings, Trainees, Training, or All for a global cycle — and choose whether it applies to a specific ship or across the board. Add a note if there is authority to record. Once open, personnel on that ship and in that class can fill and submit. Existing cycles show as Open or Closed. You have three controls on an existing cycle: Extend Closing Date when a ship needs more time, Close to stop further submissions, and Reopen where a closed cycle has to be brought back. Use extend rather than reopen where you can — it keeps a cleaner record.

Role Management decides who can approve what. Get this right before the window opens, or forms will submit and then sit with nobody able to action them. The built-in roles are the approval chain: Duty Officer, Finance Officer, Central Pay Officer, and administrator. You can also define your own role and set exactly which menus it can reach. To assign, pick the role, enter the person's service number, and set their scope. Duty Officer and Finance Officer roles are scoped to a ship. Central Pay Officer roles are scoped to a command. That scope is enforced everywhere — a Duty Officer for one ship cannot see another ship's forms, no matter how they reach the page. Revoke works the same way. When someone is drafted, revoke the old role before you assign the new one — otherwise they keep access to their previous ship.

Ships Overview lists every ship and establishment with its command and whether its form window is Open or Closed. The same screen holds the users attached to each ship and the roles they carry, filterable by role, ship and status — so you can answer "who is the Duty Officer on that ship" in seconds. Where you are setting up a ship from scratch, upload its users from a CSV file rather than entering them one by one.

Personnel Management is your record-level tool. Search by service number or name to find anyone in the system and see their ship, form year and current status. From here you can View Form to open their submission, Edit their record, or Update Contact Details where a phone number or email has changed. Service number changes are handled separately and deliberately. Enter the new service number and record the reason or authority. That reason is stored — never change a service number without it. For onboarding in volume, upload personnel from a CSV or Excel file.

Roles assigned, ships configured, personnel records clean, window open. In Part Two we follow the forms through to payroll.
